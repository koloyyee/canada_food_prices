# Canada Food Price Trend 

## Motivation
I have been a chef for over 15 years, and I have been tracing price changes ever since a decade ago after becoming the ordering person in seafood restaurant as a junior sous chef. Food price related to everyday people, and for the past 5 years we heard about the inflation makes everyone's life difficult; I want take this opportunity to use 2 datasets to find out the price trends.

### Datasets
1. Farm product prices, crops and livestock (32100077)
2. Monthly average retail prices for selected products(18100245)
