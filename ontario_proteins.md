# Ontario Monthly Protein Price Trend

### Province

Ontario

### Time frame

2022-2026

### 5 categories

1. Beef (cattle/calves ↔ beef cuts, ground beef)
2. Pork (hogs ↔ pork loin/rib/shoulder, bacon)
3. Poultry (chickens ↔ whole chicken, breasts/thighs/drumsticks)
4. Eggs (farm eggs ↔ retail egg products)
5. Dairy (farm dairy ↔ retail milk products)

## Questions

1. Which protein categories and stages (farm vs. retail) experienced the largest percentage and absolute price increases from 2022 to 2026?
2. How has the farm-to-retail price spread (Retail - Farm) and multiplier ratio (Retail/Farm) evolved, and does it point to retail margin expansion or compression?
3. Which stage (farm vs. retail) and category exhibits the highest month-over-month price volatility over the 4-year period?
4. Is farm-to-retail spread widening isolated to specific supply chains (e.g., Beef/Dairy), or is it happening uniformly across all five categories
5. When a farm-gate price shock occurs, how many months does it take to pass through to retail shelf prices across each protein category?
