# What did the meal for 2 cost, monthly, 2020–2026.

Once in while you would like to have a burger at home, but how much more or less will they cost if we make everything from the raw ingredients?

We are looking for serving:
- Cheese hamburger with ketchup, onion
- Chicken sandwich with salad greens, mayo
- Fries for 2 

# Questions:
1. Which ingredient drives the cost, and does the mix shift over time?
2. Beef burger vs. chicken sandwich — which one got relatively more expensive? (a substitution story)
3. Potatoes + oil vs. frozen fries — is scratch cheaper? Both series exist, so this is directly answerable.
4. Beef patty vs. the plant-based patty — the Meatless burgers series exists, and it's a real comparison.
5. How much is seasonal swing vs. trend? 
6. Where in Canada is this meal cheapest?
